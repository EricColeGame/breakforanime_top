3. **Outdated Server Versions:** When developers patch a game, older active servers may reject newly launched codes until the server restarts.
4. **Account Limits:** Nearly all coupons are single-use per Roblox account. If you redeemed a launch gift previously, it cannot be reused on the same profile.
