No. Your collected characters, index progression, and high-rarity inventory remain completely intact. You only need to place your units back onto your base displays to restart your passive income stream.

You should trigger your first reset as soon as you hit the $1,000,000 requirement. The immediate 1.5x coin multiplier and additional unit slot allow you to rebuild your original balance in less than a quarter of the time it took during your initial run.
