Generating raw items is only half the battle; successfully selling them at high margins drives your real growth. When preparing to take a **break for anime idle cash**, managing economic risks like state taxes and fluctuating sale values becomes essential.

### Crafting High-Margin Versus High-Volume Items
Before leaving your business unattended, you must choose between high-volume, cheap items (like basic iron daggers) or slow-crafted, premium items (such as enchanted broadswords).

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Logging Off

Even seasoned idle game veterans fall into basic traps that halt their wealth generation. Review these pitfalls to keep your production chain running at peak efficiency:

* **Leaving Incomplete Crafting Chains:** Never log off with unbalanced intermediate products. If your crafters produce sword hilts faster than blades, inventory jams will halt the entire forge.
* **Ignoring Local Tax Alerts:** Entering an offline cycle during an in-game high-tax cycle severely diminishes net margins. Shift to slow, high-value luxury goods when taxes spike.
* **Forgetting Squad Health:** Launching raids with injured or under-geared squads often results in failed runs and zero returns. Always rest or heal characters before dispatching long missions.
* **Neglecting the Offline Duration Cap:** Expanding your storage does little good if your account's base AFK timer restricts you to two hours of credit. Balance your inventory investments with offline timer expansions.
