## Maximizing Drops Per Hour (DPH)

Top-tier players rarely clear single spawns aimlessly. Instead, they calculate their "Drops Per Hour" (DPH) and construct tight farming circuits designed around respawn mechanics and area-of-effect (AoE) abilities.

### The Loop Farm Strategy

The Loop Method minimizes downtime by connecting three to four elite mob clusters or mini-bosses in a tight geographical path:

1. **Clear Spawn Alpha:** Use high-radius AoE burst skills to wipe the primary pack in under 10 seconds.
2. **Rotate to Spawn Beta:** Dash through transition zones using movement buffs to engage the second cluster.
3. **Engage Spawn Gamma:** Eliminate the third pack while maintaining your combat multipliers.
4. **Reset at Spawn Alpha:** Return to the starting point just as the initial pack respawns, keeping momentum continuous.

### Server Hopping for World Bosses

World bosses offer generous reward pools but suffer from lengthy respawn timers on a single realm. Experienced players bypass these cooldowns through server hopping. Once a boss falls, immediately switch instances to locate an active spawn. Doing this enables you to defeat world bosses four to five times more frequently per hour compared to waiting in an empty server.

## Gear Customization and Awakening Paths

Acquiring a Legendary drop is only half the battle. To extract maximum performance, take your gear through the sequential upgrade forge. Pouring materials into low-tier equipment wastes currency; reserve your rare reagents exclusively for top-tier gear.

```
Enhancement (Gold & Ore) ➔ Awakening (Soul Shards) ➔ Rerolling (Gems) ➔ Transmutation (Duplicates)
```

1. **Enhancement:** Bolsters core offensive and defensive values using common mineable ores and standard gold reserves.
2. **Awakening:** Unlocks passive perk trees via Soul Shards, adding unique procs and secondary combat triggers.
3. **Rerolling:** Modifies unfavorable sub-stats using gems until you land optimal modifiers like Critical Chance or Cooldown Reduction.
4. **Transmutation:** Consumes duplicate copies of the same item to raise star ratings, boosting all innate stats simultaneously.

| Upgrade Stage | Primary Resource Needed | Core Stat Impact | Priority Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tier 1: Base Refining | Common Iron & Gold | +10% Base ATK/DEF per level | Apply up to +5 on all usable gear |
| Tier 2: Soul Infusion | Soul Shards | Unlocks specialized trait perks | Mandatory for high-tier builds |
| Tier 3: Stat Rerolling | Premium Gems | Swaps sub-stats to meta rolls | Save exclusively for Legendary/Mythical |
| Tier 4: Transmutation | Identical Duplicate Items | Increases star tier ceiling | Complete before endgame boss runs |

Investing precious gems into standard Epic items is generally inefficient. Save high-value upgrade currencies for when you successfully complete a targeted break for anime powerful loot and secure a true endgame piece.
