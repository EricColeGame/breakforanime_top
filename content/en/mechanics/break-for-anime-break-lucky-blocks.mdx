## Evasion Strategies: Navigating Guard AI and Line of Sight

Patrolling enemies (such as Sammy or map-specific enforcers) rely on a deterministic state machine: Patrol, Alert, Pursuit, and Reset. Understanding these four behavioral phases lets you exploit pathfinding limitations and maintain a flawless extraction streak.

```
Patrol (Green) ➔ Alert (Yellow, on noise) ➔ Pursuit (Red, direct chase) ➔ Reset (Blue, returns to path)
```

### Deconstructing Enemy AI Behavior

When you perform an aggressive action, nearby patrols enter the Alert state. If you remain within their field of view during Alert, they trigger an immediate Pursuit sprint. Evading them requires breaking line of sight rather than outrunning them in a straight line, as many bosses possess continuous acceleration mechanics.

| AI State | Trigger Condition | Visual Indicator | Recommended Evasion Maneuver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Patrol** | Default idle pattern | Slow walk, neutral eyes | Move freely behind their back-cone |
| **Alert** | Block break noise nearby | Pauses, head turns to origin | Stop mining immediately; slip behind low geometry |
| **Pursuit** | Direct visual contact | Roar / High-speed charge | Execute 90-degree corner cuts around obstacles |
| **Reset** | Target lost for 4+ seconds | Walks back slowly | Re-enter the zone from an alternate flank angle |

### Key Evasion Tips
*   **Corner Cutting:** Enemy pathfinding calculates paths toward your current coordinates. Turning sharply around corners forces the AI to recalculate, shaving critical seconds off its charge speed.
*   **Elevation Changes:** Small ramps and drop-offs disorient basic patrol tracking, giving you space to sprint home.
*   **Sacrifice Play:** If trapped, drop a low-tier common item. Some AI routines register dropped loot and briefly pause, buying you an escape window.
