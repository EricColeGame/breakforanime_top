## Understanding the Limit Break System and Banner Drops

Because damage scaling heavily favors higher levels—a Level 100 character outputs roughly 9.2 times more damage than a Level 1 character—breaking past that boundary offers exponential power spikes.

However, performing these ascensions requires substantial investments, often requiring you to pause standard play to gather rare summon currencies and banner items. Many players wonder why extra materials appear when pulling on character banners; according to game mechanics documented on platforms like the [Anime Adventures Wiki](https://animeadventures.fandom.com/wiki/Frequently_Asked_Questions), items acquired from banners are rare bonus drops rewarded alongside units, rather than replacing the character drop entirely.

## How to Prepare Units for a Limit Break

You cannot simply take any unit and initiate an ascension. The game enforces strict eligibility criteria before the limit break interface unlocks its full functionality for a fighter. 

First, the target character must belong to either the **Mythic** or **Secret** rarity tier. Lower-tier units (Rare, Epic, or Legendary) serve primarily as fusion fodder or early-game stepping stones and cannot exceed the standard cap. Second, the fighter must already be at max level (Level 100). 

Gathering the required sacrifice components requires discipline. If you fail to prepare your unit inventory, you risk burning valuable evolved assets by mistake.

### Limit Break Cost Requirements

*   **Target Unit Rarity:** Mythic or Secret only
*   **Target Level:** Level 100 (Maxed base level)
*   **Fodder Material:** 12 Base Mythic units OR 6 Evolved Mythic units
*   **Catalyst Item:** 1 Wish Star

## Takedowns vs. Kills: Unlocking Evolutions and Worthiness

Before committing materials to a limit break, most characters need to be evolved into their ultimate forms. Evolution recipes frequently demand hundreds or thousands of eliminations. Understanding the distinction between a "Kill" and a "Takedown" is vital for farming these requirements without wasting entire afternoons.

*   **Kill:** A kill is only credited to the single unit that strikes the final, lethal blow on an enemy. If multiple units damage a target, only the finisher receives the kill stat.
*   **Takedown:** Takedowns function like shared participation rewards. As long as a unit inflicts any amount of damage to a mob before it dies, a takedown is registered for that character, regardless of who lands the final hit.

Because evolution criteria and Cosmic Cat stat worthiness track takedowns rather than raw final kills, you can deploy multiple units together to share credit across waves.

| Metric | Trigger Condition | Best Use Case | Mechanics Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kill** | Unit deals 100% of remaining lethal HP | Individual DPS checks | Only 1 unit gets credit per enemy |
| **Takedown** | Unit deals at least 1 hit of damage | Unit evolutions & Cat worthiness | Shared credit across all attackers |

More importantly, the **Massive Colossus** enemy archetype periodically spawns clusters of secondary minions. By setting up low-damage, wide-area-of-effect (AoE) units at the start of the track, your progression targets can clip every spawned minion, netting thousands of takedowns in a single extended session. ## Best Practices for Managing Resources and Summon Drops

Resource allocation determines how quickly your account reaches endgame viability. Summon gems, star remnants, and banner items should never be spent haphazardly.

1.  **Never Feed Evolved Mythics for Basic EXP:** Standard Legendaries and farmable EXP items should handle baseline level grinding. Reserve your Mythic duplicates strictly for evolution recipes or limit break fodder.
2.  **Dismantle Duplicate Shinies:** Shiny variants provide visually distinct cosmetic skins, but duplicates offer minimal tactical value. If you pull duplicate Shiny Mythics or Legendaries, dismantle them to harvest Star Remnants for valuable evolution materials.
3.  **Halt Summoning on Unfavorable Rotations:** If the current banner lacks the specific Dungeon Throne or event-exclusive units you need for sacrifices, stop pulling. Taking a scheduled break for anime rare drops preserves your gem economy for banners offering better returns.
4.  **Sync Infinite Runs with Daily Milestones:** Running Infinite Mode awards gems based on the wave milestones reached per world each day. Complete these runs systematically rather than pushing a single world repeatedly without claimable rewards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens when I use a limit break on an eligible unit?
Executing a limit break extends your unit's level cap by 10 levels beyond its initial Level 100 ceiling. Because high-level characters benefit from exponential damage scaling, these extra 10 levels dramatically elevate damage output, range, and overall utility in late-game modes.

### Why do I receive items instead of characters from summon banners?
Summon banners grant items as bonus drops alongside units. You will always receive a character with each pull; items appear as rare auxiliary drops to help players acquire specialized evolution or limit break catalysts without overriding standard unit summons.

### How does taking a break for anime rare drops help my account progression?
Taking a focused break for anime rare drops allows you to pause continuous story pushing to stockpile key evolution items, farm EXP items from Magic Town, and gather banner drops. This deliberate farming phase ensures your characters have the materials and level scaling needed to survive higher-difficulty raids.

### Can I limit break Legendary or Epic units?
No. The limit break feature is strictly reserved for units of **Mythic** or **Secret** rarity that have already reached the maximum base level of 100. Lower-tier units should be used as fusion material to level up your primary Mythic characters.
